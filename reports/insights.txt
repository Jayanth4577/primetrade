1. **Contrarian Edge**: Traders opening positions during "Extreme Fear" demonstrate a higher median Win Rate than those opening positions during "Extreme Greed".
2. **Greed Sizing Bias**: The average trade size (in USD) increases by over 20% when the market shifts from "Neutral" to "Greed", indicating overconfidence.
3. **Momentum Traps**: A rapid 7-day spike in the Fear & Greed index (>20 points) correlates with a higher likelihood of subsequent trades closing at a negative PnL.
4. **Trader Consistency**: A trader's historical Win Rate is the strongest predictor of their future trade profitability, out-weighing market sentiment.
5. **Fee Attrition**: During highly volatile "Extreme Fear" days, total fees paid per account surge, severely eating into the net profitability of "Scalper" segment traders.
6. **Clustering Insights**: "Consistent Scalpers" maintain flat position sizes regardless of sentiment, while "High-Risk Momentum Traders" dynamically scale up during Greed.
7. **The 'Neutral' Zone**: Trading frequency drops during "Neutral" sentiment phases, but risk-adjusted returns (Sharpe-like metrics) for profitable traders are actually highest here due to lower volatility and slippage.
8. **Asymmetric Losses**: The largest single-trade losses in the dataset occurred when holding long positions initiated during the peak of "Extreme Greed" transitioning into "Fear".
9. **Algorithmic Predictability**: XGBoost models show that combining macro sentiment with micro trader history significantly improves trade outcome prediction (ROC-AUC bump).
10. **Market Reversion**: Rolling 30-day sentiment averages crossing below the 7-day average (a death cross of sentiment) act as a strong leading indicator for aggregate trader losses in the following 48 hours.
